About

I’m an AI researcher, and founder of Tiptree Systems. I was trained as a physicist.

I design agentic systems that let more people do AI research together, and out in the open. I’m working on this because I believe AI is a foundational technology, and the knowledge to build and use it should be widely distributed. Concentrated in a few hands, it is dangerous. But I also just like research.

I spend a lot of time thinking about how AI is changing science in particular, and society in general. And where we are headed as a civilization, now that we are crossing the Rubicon into a world we share with alien minds.

Background

My PhD was on modular neural architectures, a thread my work still riffs on. Complex systems tend to be modular, and I worked on nudging neural networks in that direction with inductive biases. Before that, I used Fourier analysis to understand how deep neural networks learn. And before that, I used AI to figure out which neurons connect to which in a brain (connectomics).

I had a wholesome upbringing as a researcher, which took a village. I’m especially grateful to my advisors, who took a flyer on me: Shawn Mikula† (2013–⁠2015), Fred Hamprecht (2015–⁠2019), Yoshua Bengio (2017–⁠2024) and Bernhard Schölkopf (2019–⁠2024).
