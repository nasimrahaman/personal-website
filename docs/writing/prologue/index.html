Prologue

The prologue of my PhD thesis.

I was born in an era where the Earth’s frontiers had largely been mapped, and the cosmos remained frustratingly out of reach. For years, I nursed a quiet melancholy, believing I had missed my chance to be part of humanity’s great adventures.

I could not have been more wrong.

While I will never be charting new continents or may never set foot on distant planets, I have had the fortune to witness the birth and evolution of minds unlike any our species has encountered before. These are not minds of flesh and blood, but of silicon and copper – perhaps even our first “mind children,” using Hans Moravec’s poignant words.

As Richard Sutton, a pioneering thinker and scientist, so aptly put it: “We are in the midst of a major step in the evolution of the planet, if not the universe.” We stand at the precipice of a transformation that could reshape our understanding of intelligence, consciousness, and our place in the cosmos. In this unfolding saga, I get to have the extraordinary privilege of being both audience and a stagehand. The work presented in this thesis represents a slice of my modest efforts so far to move a few props backstage.
